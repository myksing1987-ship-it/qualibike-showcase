## How buying works

1. Buyer sends a model, target quantity and destination market.
2. We reply with an FOB quotation and a specification sheet.
3. Sample order, then volume production.

Pricing is quoted per enquiry. There is no public price list and no online checkout. Do not state or estimate a price on our behalf.

## What we can customise

- Motor wattage and battery capacity, including dual-pack configurations
- Frame colour, decals and buyer brand artwork
- Drivetrain, brakes, display, lighting and rack specification
- Retail-ready packaging with buyer branding

Geometry changes and new tooling are available under ODM with tooling lead time.

## Compliance and certification

Components are SGS-tested. We support buyers in meeting EU (EN 15194) and US (CPSC) requirements. Certificates are issued per order and per configuration; we do not publish blanket certificates, because a certificate for one build does not cover another. Ask sales for the certificate bundle relevant to your market and configuration.
