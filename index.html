What We Do

Contract manufacturing, properly run

We are not a trading company reselling someone else's bikes. Frames are sourced to our drawings, assembly and QC happen on our line, and the specification is yours.

[ICON:gear]

OEM — Your Brand

Your logo, colourways, decals and packaging on our existing platforms. Fastest route to market with the lowest tooling spend.

[ICON:box]

ODM — Your Spec

Change the frame geometry, motor, battery cell, display or drivetrain. We handle drawings, samples and revisions before tooling.

[ICON:shield]

Compliance Support

EU EN 15194 and US CPSC / UL 2849 documentation prepared with your certification body. We supply test reports, not promises.

[ICON:truck]

Logistics & Spares

Consolidated container loading, spare-parts kits shipped with each order, and a documented parts list so you can service what you sell.

Quality & Certification

What buyers actually ask us for

Procurement teams rarely ask how nice the bike looks. They ask what happens when a customer returns one. This is the framework we work to.

[ICON:check]

Incoming & In-Line QC

Battery, motor and frame inspection on arrival; functional test on every unit before packing. Records retained per batch.

[ICON:shield]

Battery Safety

Cells sourced from named suppliers with traceable batch codes. Pack-level BMS with over-charge, over-discharge and thermal cut-off.

[ICON:clock]

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Third-party inspection (SGS, BV or your nominated firm) welcomed before shipment. We schedule it into the production plan.

[ICON:box]

Warranty Terms

Standard frame and motor warranty with a defined spare-parts commitment. Exact terms confirmed per order.

How We Work

From enquiry to container

A predictable sequence, so you know what is happening and what we need from you at each step.

1

Enquiry & Spec

2

3

About Morite

Changzhou, Jiangsu — where the parts already are

Changzhou is one of China's densest e-bike component clusters. That location is the reason we can change a motor, battery or drivetrain without adding six weeks to your lead time.

Who we supply

Importers and distributors, private-label brands building a first e-bike line, and fleet or rental operators buying in volume.

What we are not

A marketplace reseller. We do not drop-ship other factories' bikes under our name, and we will tell you when a request is outside our capability.

How to start

Send the market, target price and volume. If you have a competitor product you want matched, send a photo or a spec sheet — that is usually enough.
