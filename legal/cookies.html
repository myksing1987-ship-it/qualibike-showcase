Cookie Notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This notice explains how this website uses cookies and similar storage technologies.

1. What we use

This site is intentionally built without advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. We do not run third-party analytics profiles, advertising pixels or retargeting scripts on these pages.

2. Strictly necessary storage

  • Preferences — if you select options on an enquiry form, they may be kept in your browser's local storage so your selection is not lost when you open a product page.
  • Server logs — our hosting provider records standard request data (IP address, timestamp, requested page, user agent) for security and abuse prevention.

3. Third-party content

If future pages embed third-party video or map content, those providers may set their own cookies. We will list them here before enabling any such embed.

4. Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may affect how forms behave, but will not prevent you from contacting us by email.
