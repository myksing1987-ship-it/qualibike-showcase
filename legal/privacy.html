Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Changzhou Morite Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. ("Morite", "we") collects when you contact us or use this website, and what we do with it.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us — name, company, work email, country, product interest, quantities and any message you send through the enquiry form, by email or by messaging app.

We do not ask for or store payment card details on this website.

  • To answer your enquiry and prepare a quotation or specification.

  • To protect the site against spam and abusive traffic.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your explicit request.

3. Who we share it with

Only where necessary to serve you: freight forwarders and customs brokers handling your shipment, and third-party inspection or certification bodies you or we nominate. We may also disclose information where legally required.

4. International transfers

Our operations are based in China. If you contact us from the EU, UK or elsewhere, your information will be transferred to and processed in China. We apply the same protections described here regardless of where the data is processed.

5. Retention

Enquiry records are kept while the commercial relationship is active and for as long as required by applicable record-keeping rules. Marketing enquiries that never progress are deleted after 24 months.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it for a specific purpose. Write to xufenglouis@163.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

Access to enquiry records is limited to staff who need it to respond to you. We use encrypted transport (HTTPS) for this website.

8. Children

This is a business-to-business website. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.
